FlowPath by Friven

A custom visual for Microsoft Power BI. Draws weighted arrows between origin and destination coordinates, so movement between places reads as one picture — trade lanes, shipping, migration, supply chains, network traffic.

You supply latitude and longitude directly. There is no geocoding step and no place-name lookup, so the visual plots exactly the coordinates in your model.

No network calls at runtime. The map geometry is bundled inside the visual — no map tiles are fetched, and no data leaves your report.
